# SummaRace — Answer Key (all 30 stories)

**For the researcher and the classroom teacher. Not for learners.**

Generated 2026-08-23 directly from the game's own story files (`Resources/Stories/*.json`)
through the live loader — so this document cannot drift from what the app actually asks.
30 stories, 150 reading questions, 150 SWBST elements.

## How to read this

Each story lists:

- **Main idea** — shown to the learner on the Results screen after they finish.
- **SWBST** — the five story parts. These are the researcher's own wording from the source
  document. In the **Reader** the learner meets these ideas with the story text visible; in
  the **Race** they must choose the same five parts with the text gone (the support-removal
  ladder). In **Arrange** they put these five in order. `raceFirstPickCorrect` — the child's
  FIRST choice at each of the five race gates — is the study's headline in-app measure.
- **Reading questions** — one per page, five per story, with the correct option marked.
  Only the learner's **first** answer per page is recorded (`readingFirstCorrect`).

Distractors are deliberately not listed here: they are shuffled per play and per learner, so
a printed position would be wrong. The app logs which distractor was chosen (`racePicks`), so
misconceptions can be analysed from the exported data.

## Naming

Story ids follow `sNN_<difficulty>`: `s07_hard` = Mission 7, hard. Mission = one of the ten
intervention sessions. Each mission has three stories, opened in order easy -> average -> hard.
